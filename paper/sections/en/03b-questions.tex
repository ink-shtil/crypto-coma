\section{Questions without answers}

\subsection{How does \(d\) differ from \(p\)?}
Both levels --- \(d\) and \(p\) --- lie beyond everything countable. Ask the Universe, ask
your senses, ask almost all of mathematics: about both you will hear the same answer ---
``too much''. The difference is imperceptible. And yet \(p\) is monstrously larger than
\(d\). By how much? To shrink \(p\) down to the size of \(d\) you would have to take its
logarithm \emph{twelve} times in a row. And \(d\) is already a number the digit count of
whose digit count is astronomical. Meanwhile \(p+d=p\) and \(p-d=p\) to unimaginable
precision: in the arithmetic of these giants \(d\) is a rounding error. At the same time
\(d\) is larger than anything that could be built from the atoms of the Universe. How does
one hold both thoughts at once?

\subsection{How can one work with this?}
One cannot --- if ``work'' means ``write down'' or ``compute'': there is not enough paper,
nor atoms, nor time. A mathematician works not with the number but with its \emph{address}:
the height of the tower, the growth rate, ordinals as a ruler. We compare not the numbers
but the speeds of their flight into infinity. But is tinkering with a number's address the
same as knowing the number? We confidently write \(\ccoma\) without ever having seen it.
What do we hold in our hands --- a number or a name?

\subsection{Does it exist?}
\(d\) cannot be written, cannot be reached by counting, cannot be stored. Were the Universe
to count from one forever, it would never arrive at \(d\). In what sense does \(d\) exist?
Did we invent it --- or discover it? If it is a fact about the world, where does that fact
reside? If only about our notation --- why does it obey the same laws as the real numbers?

\subsection{A list of questions}
\begin{itemize}
  \item If both \(d\) and \(p\) are ``infinitely large'' to a human, is there a difference
        between them --- or does it live only on paper?
  \item A number that can be neither written, nor computed, nor reached by counting --- does
        it exist?
  \item Did we invent the crypto coma --- or stumble upon it?
  \item Is working with a number's address the same as working with the number?
  \item ``Too small'' --- too small for what? What does size add that meaning lacks?
  \item Is there a last number --- or does thought have no ceiling?
  \item Why are we drawn up this ladder at all?
\end{itemize}
